GRINGOTTS TRADING JOURNAL
=========================

1. Drag "Gringotts Trading Journal" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.

For Apple Silicon Macs only (M1 or later), macOS 13 or newer.
It does not run on Intel Macs.


IF MACOS SAYS THE APP "CANNOT BE OPENED" OR "CANNOT BE VERIFIED"
----------------------------------------------------------------
The app is not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS asks you to
approve it once. Use ONE of these:

A) System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the Security section,
   find the message about "Gringotts Trading Journal" and click "Open Anyway".
   Enter your password, then click "Open".
   (On recent macOS, right-click > Open no longer works.)

B) Open Terminal and run this, then open the app normally:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Gringotts Trading Journal.app"

You only need to do this the first time.

Your data stays on your Mac. The app never sends it anywhere.
